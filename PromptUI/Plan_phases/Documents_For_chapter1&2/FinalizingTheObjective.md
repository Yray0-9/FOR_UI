## Objectives of the Study

The primary objective of this study is to develop SafeBooks, a web-based client financial records monitoring system that centralizes client records, supports compliance tracking, and provides financial analytics with an evaluated forecasting model for bookkeeping review and follow-up.

Specifically, the study aims to:

1. To train, test, and compare Moving Average, Weighted Moving Average, and Linear Regression forecasting models using historical client financial records, and evaluate their performance using MAE, MAPE, and RMSE.

2. To design and develop a web-based workspace for managing client business profiles and related bookkeeping records.

3. To implement structured period-based financial record entry for manually encoded sales, expenses, tax-related items, deadlines, and notes.

4. To provide monitoring support for client activity, filing status, remarks, and upcoming compliance deadlines.

5. To generate financial analytics that summarize recorded financial data, present trends, and integrate the selected Weighted Moving Average forecasting model according to monthly, quarterly, or annual transaction schedules.

6. To present dashboard and report outputs that consolidate financial summaries, compliance status, and forecasting results for bookkeeper review and follow-up.
